# Architecture Decision Record

Use this structure for durable governance and architecture decisions.

## Decision gate

State the decision that requires accountable review.

## Context

Record the facts and constraints that make the decision necessary.

## Decision

Describe the selected approach.

## Owns

List the contracts and behavior governed by this decision.

## Does not own

List adjacent concerns that remain outside this decision.

## Consequences

Record benefits, costs, compatibility effects, and operational limits.

## Alternatives considered

Record credible alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Implementation plan

Describe the smallest reviewable implementation slices.

## Validation

Separate deterministic validation from advisory human judgment.

## Rollback or replacement

Describe how a later decision can supersede this one.
