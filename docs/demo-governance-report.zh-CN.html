如何阅读这份报告

把报告当作审阅地图，而不是批准证书。

  1. 1
    先看当前状态

    了解多少确定性检查通过、警告或失败，以及多少问题仍需人工判断。

  2. 2
    再看需要关注的事项

    按照每条发现的具体下一步和处理区域，指定负责人。

  3. 3
    最后审阅全部发现

    在决定合并、发布或部署前，阅读准确检查项、仓库事实和范围限制。

需要关注什么

把非通过发现转换成有明确责任的下一步。

  1. WARN
    governance:placeholders

    下一步： Replace each repository placeholder or explicitly defer it with an owner.

    处理位置： AGENTS.md, ADR/invariant files and capability manifests
  2. WARN
    references:example-capability:evaluation

    下一步： Declare the capability's evaluation bundle or retain an honest needs_seed_cases state.

    处理位置： the capability manifest and its evaluation/ bundle
  3. WARN
    evaluation:evaluation/example-capability

    下一步： Add or review seed, golden and failure cases until the declared readiness is supported.

    处理位置： the evaluation bundle named by the finding
  4. ADVISORY
    governance:human-review

    下一步： Confirm the real approval and escalation boundary with an accountable owner.

    处理位置： a repository ADR or equivalent decision record

工作方式

自动化检查仓库事实，人保留判断和授权。

01 | DECLAREPolicy, capability, owner and risk
02 | CONNECTSources, schemas and evidence
03 | VERIFYContracts, readiness and drift
04 | DECIDEHuman review and explicit authority

ADVISORY 代表什么
